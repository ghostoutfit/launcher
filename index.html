How to use Launcher

Launching the marble

Launch position
Where the marble starts on the track. Slide it anywhere from 0 to 15 meters (m).
Launch speed
How fast the marble starts. Slide it from 0 to 10 meters per second (m/s). The marble always starts out rolling to the right.

Press Launch to let the marble go.

Track pieces

Drag a piece from the Track pieces box onto the track, or click a piece and then click the track. Drag a piece to move it. Click a piece and press the red × (or the Backspace key) to take it off.

±Speed change
Adds to the marble’s speed or takes some away. Use the − and + buttons on its sign to change the amount. Each click changes it by 2.
Target speed
Sets the marble to the speed on its sign. Use − and + to choose the speed. A speed below zero sends the marble back toward the start. You can also turn on its timer: the marble waits inside until that time. Use the timer’s − and + to change the time.
▦Friction pad
A rough patch on the track. Drag either end of it to make it longer or shorter.
█Wall / Bounce
Stops the marble. Click the wall to change it to a bouncy wall, and click it again to change it back.
Marble 2 (M2)
A second marble that sits still until the first marble hits it. You can only put one on the track.

Position map

Press Position Map to put a dot on the track where the marble is at each whole second: 0 s, 1 s, 2 s, and so on.

Making your own puzzles

  1. Open the Create tab. Build a track and press Launch.
  2. Choose which graph the puzzle uses: Position, Distance or Velocity.
  3. Press Make puzzle. A puzzle code appears in the box.

There are two ways to play a puzzle code:

Paste it in the box
Press Copy and send the code to someone. They open the Create tab, paste the code into the box, and press Load & play. When they’re done, Back to building brings back their own track.
Add it to the web address
Paste the code onto the end of this page’s address in the browser’s address bar, then press Enter. The puzzle opens right away. You can share that whole address as a link.
